🎓 Data Science & Machine Learning Methodology

1. Real Historical Dataset

Trained on 5,524 matches from England Premier League, Spain La Liga, Italy Serie A, Germany Bundesliga, and France Ligue 1 with zero synthetic rows.

2. Zero Future Leakage

Rolling xG averages use strict .shift(1) lag. Pre-match predictions only utilize stats that existed prior to kickoff.

Achieves 51% - 52% 3-way multi-class accuracy, substantially exceeding random guessing baseline (33.3%).
